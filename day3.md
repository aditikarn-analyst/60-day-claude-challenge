# Day 3: Role-Based Prompting

## What I Learned

Role-Based Prompting is a technique where you assign Claude a specific role or persona before asking a question. Instead of receiving generic responses, you get answers from the perspective of a domain expert.

### Key Learnings

### 1. Persona-Driven Responses
Different roles produce different styles of thinking, analysis, and recommendations.

**Example:**
- Developer → Focuses on technical solutions
- Product Manager → Focuses on priorities and strategy
- Marketer → Focuses on audience and growth
- Founder → Focuses on business impact

### 2. Higher Quality Outputs
Role prompts help Claude generate more relevant, detailed, and specialized responses compared to generic prompts.

### 3. Better Decision Making
By asking the same question from multiple roles, you can evaluate problems from different professional perspectives and make more informed decisions.

### 4. Real-World Simulation
Claude can simulate various professionals, including:
- Founder
- Developer
- Product Manager
- HR Manager
- Marketer
- Consultant
- Teacher
- Data Analyst

## Key Takeaway

The quality of AI responses improves significantly when you tell Claude who it should be before telling it what to do.

### Simple Formula

Role + Context + Goal + Format = Better AI Responses

## Example

### Generic Prompt
How can I improve my startup?

### Role-Based Prompt
Act as a startup founder and growth consultant. Analyze my startup and suggest three strategies to increase customer acquisition within the next six months.

The second prompt produces more focused, actionable, and expert-level recommendations.
